Title: Operational Procedure and Safety Protocol for Molding Press-04
Machine ID: Press-04

1. Machine Specifications & Parameters
- Target Temperature: 170.0°C (Nominal Range: 165.0°C - 175.0°C)
- Maximum Safe Operating Temperature: 180.0°C
- Target Hydraulic Pressure: 70.0 PSI (Nominal Range: 65.0 - 73.0 PSI)
- Line Speed: 42.0 m/min

2. High Temperature Alert Procedure
- WARNING: Operating Press-04 above 180.0°C requires mandatory supervisor sign-off and 100% visual sample inspection.
- High operating temperatures (e.g. 184°C - 188°C) significantly impair resin cooling cycles, resulting in surface cracks, localized warping, and structural weakness in bottle, metal nut, and screw components.
- In case of temperature spike > 180°C:
  a. Immediately inspect thermal control loop thermocouple on Zone 2.
  b. Lower line speed to 38 m/min to allow auxiliary cooling.
  c. Initiate RAG context lookup for recent thermal defect incidents on Press-04.
