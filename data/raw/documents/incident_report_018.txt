Document Type: Historical Incident Report
Document ID: INC-018
Date: 2026-08-14
Machine ID: Press-04
Batch ID: RB-1988
Defect Type: Surface Crack
Operating Temp: 185.2°C

Incident Description:
Batch RB-1988 experienced a high defect rejection rate (118 rejected units out of 500) due to severe surface micro-cracking. Analysis confirmed that elevated operating temperature of Press-04 (185.2°C) caused thermal expansion strain in plasticized resin.

Lessons Learned & Recommendation:
When Press-04 operates at or above 184°C, surface crack defect rate exponentially increases. Recommend inspecting cooling water lines and re-calibrating temperature sensors immediately whenever temperatures exceed 180°C.
