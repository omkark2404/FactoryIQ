Title: Standard Quality Management & Defect Escalation Guidelines

1. Purpose and Scope
This document outlines standard quality inspection thresholds, defect classification standards, and mandatory escalation protocols across all production lines including Bottle, Screw, Metal Nut, and Tile molding processes.

2. Temperature Control & Defect Thresholds
- Standard operating temperature range for Press series machines (Press-01 to Press-04) is 165°C to 178°C.
- Operating temperatures exceeding 180°C present a critical thermal risk. Elevated temperatures induce material thermal expansion, localized stress, and surface cracking.
- If a press machine operates above 180°C for more than 10 consecutive minutes, the current production batch MUST be flagged for immediate visual anomaly inspection.

3. Defect Classification Standard
- Surface Anomaly / Micro-cracks: High Severity. Triggers mandatory batch quarantine if defect rate exceeds 1.5%.
- Dimensional Abnormality: Medium Severity. Requires calibration check of hydraulic pressure clamps.
- Texture Deviation: Low Severity. Standard surface finish inspection required.

4. Escalation Protocol
When a batch is flagged with HIGH Risk (Rejection Risk > 70% or Anomaly Score > 0.85):
Step 1: Halt automated packaging for the batch.
Step 2: Cross-reference machine operating temperature against SOP.
Step 3: Review historical incident logs for the active machine ID.
Step 4: Conduct visual sampling using the automated vision inspection system on at least 50 random units.
